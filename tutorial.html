平台使用教程

第一步：在交易所创建并获取 API Key

Log in to your exchange account (Binance, OKX, etc.), navigate to the API management section, create a new API Key, and ensure you enable "Reading" and "Spot Trading" permissions. Do NOT enable withdrawal permissions.

第二步：在控制台绑定 API

Go to the Galaxy Quantitative console, click on "Add New API", select your exchange, and securely paste your API Key and Secret. Our platform uses enterprise-grade encryption to store your keys.

第三步：选择心仪交易员，设置风控并启动跟单

Browse our list of top-tier traders, select one that matches your risk appetite, configure your investment amount and stop-loss limits, and start copying their trades instantly.
